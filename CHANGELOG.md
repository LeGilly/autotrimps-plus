# Changelog

## 0.2.0 · 2026-11-25

- AT+ now plays Universe 1 by itself, with an **AutoTrimps Plus** switch at the top of AutoTrimps' Core tab (on by default; off gives AutoTrimps back to you as it is).
- Picks every run: challenges that unlock something come first (Discipline, Metal, Size, Scientist I–V, Meditate, Decay, Trimp, Trapper, Frugal, Mapocalypse, Coordinate, Slow, Mapology, Devastation), once your highest zone is 10 past where the challenge ends, and end the moment they're completed. Then Dailies (one about to expire goes first), then normal runs in the best repeatable helium challenge your total helium allows, using the players' thresholds (Electricity 100K, Life 1M, Crushed 3M, Nom 10M, Toxicity 35M, Lead 80M with Headstart I, Corrupted 150M, Domination 500M), ending when helium per hour drops, after the void maps.
- An unlock run stuck on a zone for 2 hours ends as a normal run; that challenge waits 10 more zones.
- Buys Masteries as soon as they're affordable, in the players' recommended order, with Headstart I–II and Hyperspeed I–II rushed (Hyperspeed I is the 10th Mastery instead of the 19th).
- Keeps 77 AutoTrimps settings at AutoTrimps' own recommended values (perks and preset swap, Gene Assist, Scryer, map bonus, voids, portal buffers, the generator and magmite, Spire, Dailies, challenge settings). They are outlined in AutoTrimps' window; a change to one of them is put back while AT+ is on. Visual settings are never touched.
- Says in the log what this run is, how it ends and what comes next.
- Waits (and says so) in Universe 2: Universe 1 only for now.

## 0.1.1 · 2026-11-25

- The "AutoTrimps Plus is running" line now shows when the game starts with offline time too: AutoTrimps hides untyped log lines during a time warp, so it waits until the warp is over.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-25

- First version: starts AutoTrimps (SadAugust v7.2.8, unmodified) from the mod folder instead of the internet, then AutoTrimps Plus. No automation of its own yet.
- Says in the game log that AutoTrimps Plus is running, with both versions.
